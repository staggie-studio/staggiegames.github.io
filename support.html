Support

For game support, purchase issues, bug reports, or privacy requests, email contact@staggiegames.com.

What to Include

  • The game name.
  • Your platform: iOS or Android.
  • Your player ID or platform identifier, if available.
  • Your device model and operating-system version.
  • A short description of the issue and any relevant screenshots.
  • For purchase issues, the platform receipt or transaction ID. Never send full payment-card details.

Privacy and Data Deletion Requests

Staggie Games does not currently provide a standalone user account. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information under our control, visit Privacy Choices or email us with the subject “Privacy Request”.
