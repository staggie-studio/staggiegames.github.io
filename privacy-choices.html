Privacy Choices

Use this page to learn how to manage advertising choices and submit a privacy request relating to Staggie Games.

Data Access, Correction, or Deletion

We do not currently offer a standalone Staggie Games account. You may still request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information under our control.

Email contact@staggiegames.com with the subject “Privacy Request”. Include:

  • The relevant game.
  • Your platform: iOS or Android.
  • Your player ID, app-instance ID, or platform identifier, if visible in the game.
  • The type of request: access, correction, deletion, objection, or consent withdrawal.

Do not send passwords or full payment-card details. We may ask for additional information to verify and locate the data.

Submit Privacy Request

Advertising and Tracking Choices

  • Use any privacy or consent controls shown inside the game.
  • On iOS, manage tracking permissions in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • On Android, use the advertising and privacy controls available in your device settings.
  • You may reset or limit the use of your device advertising identifier through platform settings, where available.

Opting out of personalized advertising may result in non-personalized ads rather than removing ads entirely.

Platform Data

Google Play Games and Apple Game Center are operated by Google and Apple. Platform-profile data, achievements, and platform account settings should be managed through the relevant Google or Apple account controls.
