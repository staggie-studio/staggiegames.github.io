Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how STAGGIE GAMES YAZILIM VE TEKNOLOJİ ANONİM ŞİRKETİ (“Staggie Games,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, shares, retains, and protects information when you use our mobile games, websites, and related services (collectively, the “Services”). It applies to Staggie Games products generally. A particular Service may not use every provider or feature listed below.

We do not currently offer a standalone Staggie Games user-account system. Google Play Games and Apple Game Center, where enabled, are platform services managed by Google and Apple.

1. Data Controller and Contact

The data controller is STAGGIE GAMES YAZILIM VE TEKNOLOJİ ANONİM ŞİRKETİ. Privacy questions and requests may be sent to contact@staggiegames.com.

2. Information We Collect and How We Collect It

Information you provide directly

  • Your email address and the contents of messages you send to support, privacy, or business channels.
  • Information you voluntarily submit through feedback forms, surveys, beta tests, or promotions, if offered.
  • Purchase receipts or transaction identifiers that you choose to provide when requesting purchase support. Do not send full payment-card details.

Information collected automatically through the app and integrated SDKs

  • Gameplay and usage data: game progress, levels, sessions, interactions, feature usage, virtual-item events, and gameplay events.
  • Device and app data: device model, operating system, app version, language, country or approximate region, time zone, network and performance information.
  • Identifiers: app-instance or installation identifiers, advertising identifiers where available and permitted, and platform identifiers where a platform feature is enabled.
  • Diagnostics: crash logs, error reports, stack traces, and related technical information.
  • Advertising and attribution data: ad impressions, ad interactions, installs, campaign and referral information, conversion events, consent status, and related measurement data.

Purchases

Payments are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not receive your full payment-card information. We may receive a transaction identifier, purchased product, price or currency, purchase status, and receipt information needed to deliver or verify the purchase and handle support.

Website

As of the effective date, our public website does not use Staggie Games advertising cookies or website analytics. Our hosting and email providers may process basic technical logs, IP addresses, and communications as necessary to operate and secure those services.

3. Why We Use Information

  • To provide, maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the Services.
  • To save or synchronize game functionality where supported by the platform.
  • To deliver and verify purchases.
  • To analyze gameplay, balancing, retention, performance, and product quality.
  • To detect crashes, bugs, fraud, abuse, cheating, and security incidents.
  • To measure marketing performance and provide ads, including personalized ads only where permitted and consented to.
  • To respond to support, privacy, and business inquiries.
  • To comply with law and enforce our Terms of Service.

4. Legal Bases

Where a legal basis is required, we rely on one or more of the following:

  • Contract: processing needed to provide game functionality or purchases you request.
  • Legitimate interests: operating, securing, debugging, measuring, and improving the Services, balanced against your rights.
  • Consent: personalized advertising, tracking, or other processing where consent is required.
  • Legal obligation: processing required by applicable law, accounting, tax, fraud-prevention, or lawful authority requests.

Information is collected electronically through the app, integrated SDKs, platform services, and communications you send to us.

Where required, we obtain consent before using data for personalized advertising or tracking. 7. Sharing of Information

We may share information with:

  • Analytics, attribution, crash-reporting, hosting, security, advertising, mediation, email, and support providers for the purposes described above.
  • Apple, Google, and other platform or payment providers for distribution, purchases, platform features, fraud prevention, and support.
  • Professional advisers and public authorities where required by law or necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.
  • A successor or potential successor in connection with a merger, financing, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of assets, subject to appropriate safeguards.

We do not sell personal information for money. Certain advertising or measurement activities may be treated as “sharing,” “targeted advertising,” or similar concepts under some privacy laws. Where required, we provide consent or opt-out choices.

8. Retention and Deletion

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including operating and securing the Services, completing purchases, responding to requests, complying with law, resolving disputes, and enforcing agreements. Retention depends on the data category, provider configuration, legal requirements, and whether the data has been aggregated or de-identified.

  • Support and privacy correspondence is retained as needed to resolve the request and document our response.
  • Analytics, attribution, advertising, and diagnostic data is retained according to our configured provider settings and operational needs, then deleted or aggregated.
  • Transaction and accounting records may be retained for periods required by tax, accounting, consumer-protection, or fraud-prevention law.
  • Backups may retain deleted information for a limited period until they are overwritten through normal backup cycles.

To request access, correction, or deletion of personal information under our control, use our Privacy Choices page or email contact@staggiegames.com. We may request information needed to verify and locate your data. Data managed solely by Apple, Google, or another platform provider must be managed through that provider’s account and privacy controls.

9. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information, including access controls and encrypted transmission where appropriate. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

10. International Transfers

Our service providers may process information outside your country. Where required, we use lawful transfer mechanisms, contractual safeguards, and other measures required by applicable data-protection law.

11. Children

Our Services are intended for a general audience and are not designed or directed primarily to children under 13, or the minimum digital-consent age in the relevant country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided information improperly, contact us so we can review and, where appropriate, delete it.

12. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, obtain information about, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or receive a portable copy of certain personal information, and to withdraw consent. You may also have the right to complain to a competent data-protection authority.
